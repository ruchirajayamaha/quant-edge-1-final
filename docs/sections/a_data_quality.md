## 2. Session-Grid Alignment, Boundary Guards, Quarantine & Missing-Data Discipline

1. **Deterministic NYSE session grid & requested boundary coverage:** Raw ETF observations are aligned onto the expected NYSE regular trading-session calendar (`expected_nyse_sessions`), accounting for all ten regular NYSE holiday rules (including NYSE Rule 7.2 for Saturday New Year's Day and Juneteenth observance beginning in 2022) plus the five documented unplanned/mourning full-day closures since 2005 (`2007-01-02`, `2012-10-29`, `2012-10-30`, `2018-12-05`, `2025-01-09`). `build_session_price_grid` validates first and last expected NYSE sessions against `requested_start` / `requested_end` (and never clips an explicit `calendar_sessions` back to observed endpoints), handling requested weekend/holiday bounds cleanly while rejecting truncated downloads (`DataUnavailableError`). Across `[2005-01-03, 2026-10-30]`, `n_leading_missing_expected_sessions = 0`, `n_trailing_missing_expected_sessions = 0`, and `n_interior_missing_expected_sessions = 0`.
2. **Unexpected non-NYSE session quarantine:** Observed prices are reindexed strictly onto expected NYSE sessions (never unioned with unexpected vendor weekend/holiday dates). Any unexpected non-NYSE dates are preserved in `etf_prices_raw.parquet` and `quarantined_unexpected_prices.parquet` with `rule = 'unexpected_non_nyse_session'` and `action = 'quarantine_exclude_from_modelling_grid'` (or rejected if `unexpected_session_policy = 'error'`) so they cannot contaminate one-session returns. In the active snapshot, `n_quarantined_unexpected_sessions = 0`.
3. **One-session returns before incomplete-row exclusion:** Returns are computed on the uncollapsed NYSE session grid via `P_t / P_{t-1} - 1` with **zero implicit forward-fill** and **no artificial first-day zero** (`2005-01-03` serves solely as the initial price anchor). Any missing price at session `t` invalidates *both* adjacent intervals (`t-1 -> t` and `t -> t+1`) before incomplete return rows are excluded and logged in `excluded_return_rows.parquet`. In the active snapshot, `n_missing_price_cells = 0` and `n_excluded_return_rows = 0` (`uninterrupted_return_sessions = True`).
4. **Decision-cutoff vs. ex-post risk-free alignment (`DTB3`):** `DTB3` quotes (annual percent, 360-day bank-discount basis) are converted to the daily fractional approximation `(rate_pct / 100.0) / 252` with zero backfill (`no_backfill = True`) under two explicitly distinguished series:
   - **Forward-decision proxy (`rf_daily.parquet`, policy `r3_versioned_release_evidence_day_level`):** The decision cutoff for return session `t` remains the preceding NYSE close (`16:00:00 America/New_York`). Each DTB3 observation version in `dtb3_rf_versions.parquet` becomes usable at `16:15:00 America/New_York` on its availability date. For the daily-vintage era (first vintage on or after `2006-02-23`) that date is the latest of an evidence calendar, the ALFRED vintage date, and any documented omission date. At each cutoff, `select_rf_versions_asof` takes the most recent eligible observation, then that observation's latest eligible version, so a late revision to an older observation cannot replace a newer quote. `rf_provenance.parquet` records the cutoff, selected observation, value, version, and evidence for every return session. All `5,491` return sessions have a value, with zero backfill.
     - **Evidence classes:** `documented` (Federal Reserve notices: `21` no-release days and `4` omitted Treasury releases), `inferred` (`6` no-release days from ALFRED absence alone, which are not independently proven Board closures, plus observations whose first vintage is later than the calendar), `unresolved` (`2` observations in December 2025, where the ALFRED release list and DTB3 vintages disagree), and `calendar_fallback` (sparse history).
     - **Value versions:** All `5,142` published vintages were stitched globally, with `0` inexact dates. Revisions in scope are `3` observations corrected on `2017-11-13` and `1` withdrawn value (`2011-04-22`). Decisions in 2005 use the archived `2.29%` for `2005-01-07`, not the 2017 correction to `2.28%`. All `323` archived weekly H.15 values from the sparse era match the first ALFRED vintage.
     - **Sparse history:** `314` observations with first vintages before `2006-02-23` keep the schedule calendar and a `sparse_fallback` label. They are never delayed to the June 2005 first vintage.
     - **Change from `r2`:** `1,125` of `5,491` sessions changed value. Signed effect `+0.066` bp per year, absolute effect `0.84` bp per year, largest quote change `81` bp (`2008-09-22`), largest one-session return change `0.32` bp. Signed averages hide offsetting changes and are not worst-case bounds. Nearly all of this comes from the inferred vintage floor in 2006-2012, which may over-delay observations.
   - **Contemporaneous ex-post proxy (`rf_daily_ex_post.parquet`):** Matches `obs_date <= t` strictly as an ex-post reference (`4.00% / 100 / 252` on `2026-10-30`), tagged `ex_post_contemporaneous_proxy_not_for_forward_decisions`; differs from `rf_daily` on `4,393` of `5,491` return sessions.
   - **Conversion sensitivity (`rf_daily_accrual_91d_sensitivity.parquet`):** A labelled 91-day bill accrual on the same selected quotes, assuming a fixed term. It runs about `4.1` bp per year above `/252` over the full sample and `8.0` bp per year over 2021-2026. `/252` remains primary, and neither is an exact realized Treasury return.
5. **Cached index verification:** Snapshot cache loading verifies all file SHA-256 digests and validates serialized `rf_daily.parquet`, `rf_daily_ex_post.parquet`, and `log_returns.parquet` `DatetimeIndex` values directly against `simple_returns.index` without positional index overwriting.

## 3. Simple vs. Log Return Conventions

- **Simple returns (`simple_returns`):** Portfolio return at horizon `t` aggregates linearly across pre-return holdings weights in simple returns:
  $$R_{p,t} = \sum_{i=1}^{7} w_{i,t-} R_{i,t}, \qquad L_t = -R_{p,t}$$
  Because \(\ln(1 + x)\) is strictly concave, \(\sum_i w_{i,t-} \ln(1 + R_{i,t}) \neq \ln(1 + R_{p,t})\) by Jensen's inequality. Simple returns are therefore mandatory for cross-sectional portfolio P&L aggregation and committee simple-loss VaR/ES comparisons.
- **Log returns (`log_returns = log1p(simple_returns)`):** Multi-period compounding is additive in log returns (\(\ln(P_T / P_0) = \sum_{t=1}^T r_t\)), so statistical time-series diagnostics (ADF unit-root tests, correlation tables, and volatility-regime screening) are evaluated on `log_returns`.

## 4. Quality Flagging & Stationarity Findings

Flagging (`quality_flags.parquet`) audits extreme moves and regime shifts while **retaining 100% of valid NYSE return observations** (`action = 'retain'`):

- **`stale_flat_return_run` (`>= 3` consecutive zero returns):** `0` flags across all 7 ETFs.
- **`large_negative_return` (`simple_return <= -0.05`):** `188` flags across all 7 ETFs (`EEM`: 51, `XLE`: 51, `EFA`: 31, `XLK`: 23, `SPY`: 18, `GLD`: 13, `AGG`: 1), led by `XLE` (`-20.14%` on `2020-03-09`) and `EEM` (`-16.17%` on `2008-10-15`).
- **`robust_mad_jump` (`|r - median| / (1.4826 * MAD) > 8.0`, with safe `MAD = 0` fallback):** `101` flags across all 7 ETFs (`SPY`: 23, `EFA`: 18, `XLE`: 16, `EEM`: 15, `AGG`: 13, `XLK`: 10, `GLD`: 6). Retaining these genuine crisis observations is essential for tail-risk (`VaR`, `ES`, `EVT`) realism.
- **`candidate_structural_break` (causal trailing 63-day vs. prior non-overlapping 252-day log-return volatility ratio `>= 2.5`, 63-day refractory window):** `18` flags across 6 ETFs (`SPY`: 5, `EEM`: 3, `XLE`: 3, `XLK`: 3, `AGG`: 2, `EFA`: 2, `GLD`: 0), clustering around October 2008 (GFC), October 2011 (Eurozone/US downgrade), March–April 2018, March–June 2020 (COVID-19 liquidity shock), and April 2025. These are heuristic regime-shift diagnostics, **not** formal proof of permanent structural breaks.
- **ADF stationarity diagnostics (`adf_stationarity.csv`):** All seven ETFs' daily log-return series strongly reject the unit-root null at the 1% and 5% levels (`ADF` statistics between `-14.42` for `XLE` and `-75.11` for `GLD`, all `p <= 7.86e-27`), alongside severe excess kurtosis (`6.55` in `GLD` up to `55.33` in `AGG`).

## 5. Data & Diagnostic Limitations

1. **Stationarity vs. i.i.d.:** Rejecting an ADF unit root confirms level/mean stationarity (`I(0)`) of daily returns; it does **not** imply i.i.d. returns, Gaussian tails, or constant conditional variance. Volatility clustering and heavy tails motivate unconditional POT-GPD EVT and rolling out-of-sample backtesting in Steps 5–7. (Stationarity of the *actual modelled portfolio return series* will be tested separately in Step 4 once Step 3 weights are constructed.)
2. **`DTB3` daily proxy and day-level release evidence:** Dividing a 360-day bank-discount annual percentage rate by `252` is an approximate daily risk-free proxy rather than an exact realized 1-day Treasury bill holding-period return. Availability evidence is day-level: historical intraday first-release times are not verified, and ALFRED vintage dates can trail or precede the source release. Sparse history before `2006-02-23` is a labelled calendar fallback, and first values before the first vintage rest on archived weekly H.15 pages rather than a confirmed first release. `rf_daily` is not exact historical point-in-time data. The earlier `r2` series was a schedule proxy rather than a conservative series: it missed Board closures and omitted Treasury data.
3. **US ETF close vs. non-synchronous foreign underlying hours (`EFA`, `EEM`):** All seven ETFs trade synchronously on US exchanges until `16:00 America/New_York`, matching US portfolio execution; however, `EFA` and `EEM` track foreign equity markets whose local cash sessions close earlier, so daily ETF returns and cross-correlations reflect US afternoon ETF price discovery rather than synchronous local cash-index closes.
4. **Adjusted-close execution simplification:** `yfinance` `auto_adjust=True` assumes frictionless reinvestment of cash distributions on ex-dates without withholding taxes, bid-ask spreads, or intraday execution slippage.
