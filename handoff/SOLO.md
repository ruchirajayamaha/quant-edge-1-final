## Step 6 Summary — A3 Out-of-Sample Rolling VaR Backtesting, Coverage Tests & 250-Day Basel Diagnostic

1. **Strictly Out-of-Sample Rolling VaR Backtesting Engine (`src/quantedge/backtest.py`):**
   - `rolling_backtest(asset_returns, weights_before, forecast_fn, *, confidence, window, seed=None) -> pd.DataFrame`: returns `BACKTEST_COLUMNS` across all expected NYSE trading sessions between `weights_before.index[0]` and `weights_before.index[-1]`. Preserves full prior `asset_returns` history before `weights_before.index[0]` for training warmup (`2005-01-04..2007-01-04`, `504` sessions `>= 500`), slices the exact `window` preceding NYSE sessions (`train_end < t`), forecasts 1-day risk via generic `RiskForecastFn` at pre-return exposure `weights_before.loc[t]`, evaluates realized 1-day pre-cost gross market loss `L_t = -(asset_returns.loc[t] @ weights_before.loc[t])` at the exact same exposure, defines strict exceedance `realized_loss > forecast_var` (equality is not an exception; negative VaR preserved), retains invalid/skipped rows with `status='unavailable'`, explicit `reason`, and `exceedance=None`, and reuses `seed` deterministically at each origin (common random numbers) so appending future targets never alters earlier forecasts.
2. **Kupiec POF, Christoffersen Independence, Conditional Coverage & Educational Basel Traffic-Light (`src/quantedge/_backtest_diagnostics.py`, `src/quantedge/backtest.py`):**
   - `coverage_tests(backtest_rows) -> pd.DataFrame`: returns `COVERAGE_TEST_COLUMNS` grouped by `(model, confidence)` (rejecting mixed portfolios or duplicate dates). Evaluates all-$N$ Kupiec POF (`df=1`) with exact `0 * log(0) = 0` boundary arithmetic, counts consecutive-NYSE-session Markov transitions `(n00, n01, n10, n11)` without bridging across skipped or missing sessions, evaluates first-state-conditioned Christoffersen independence (`df=1`) and conditional coverage (`LR_cc = LR_ind + LR_uc_transition`, `df=2`) when identifiable on a single contiguous valid run (`valid_runs == 1`), and withholds `LR_ind` / `LR_cc` (`None`) with explicit diagnostic reasons when origin/outcome states are unvisited or valid runs are disconnected.
   - `basel_traffic_light(backtest_rows_99) -> dict[str, Any]`: evaluates the educational 250-consecutive-NYSE-session 99% 1-day diagnostic (`green` 0–4, `yellow` 5–9, `red` >=10; expected `2.5` exceptions) at the fixed latest supplied target date without dropping gaps or searching backward for an older block.

## Step 5 Summary — C1 Historical, Parametric Normal & Monte Carlo VaR + Expected Shortfall (+ `R1–R2` Bounded Review Fixes & Visible Exports)

1. **Shared Empirical Tail-Weight & Historical VaR/ES Engine (`src/quantedge/es.py`):**
   - `empirical_tail_weights(losses, confidence) -> pd.Series`: integrates the empirical quantile distribution over the exact worst `alpha = 1 - confidence` probability mass (`m = n * alpha` observations). Uses the descending order-statistic boundary `u_* = L_{[ceil(m)]}` (`sorted_asc[n - ceil(m)]`), assigning `1 / m` to strict exceedances `L_i > u_*` (`k_strict < m`) and splitting the remaining fractional/tied tail mass `(m - k_strict) / (m * k_tie)` equally across all `k_tie >= 1` boundary ties `L_i == u_*`. Preserves `losses.index` and sums to `1.0` (`abs(sum - 1.0) <= 1e-15`).
   - `empirical_var_and_es(losses, confidence)`: defines Historical VaR via the empirical step-CDF quantile `VaR_c = F_n^{-1}(c) = L_{(ceil(n * c))}` (`method="inverted_cdf"`) and Historical ES as `float(tail_weights @ losses)`, guaranteeing `ES_c >= VaR_c`, confidence monotonicity, and exact cross-asset Euler historical ES additivity.

## Step 3 Summary — B1 Portfolio Construction & Drifting Return Paths

1. **Core candidate rules & single shared path engine (`src/quantedge/portfolio.py`):**

Infeasible constraints or failed solver candidates raise `PortfolioOptimizationError` (`ValueError`) and are **never** repaired via silent clipping or renormalization.
