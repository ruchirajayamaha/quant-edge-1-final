C2 - UNCONDITIONAL POT-GPD EVT
Fit losses above threshold u: excess=loss-u conditional on loss>u, scipy.stats.genpareto.fit(excess, floc=0). State location, scale and support conventions. Core is unconditional EVT, with dependence/volatility limitations disclosed.

Threshold grid: configure a documented diagnostic grid and selected-threshold policy consistently; runtime's current 0.95 is a provisional selected value, not a command to ignore diagnostics. Values such as 85/87.5/90/92.5/95 may be assessed, but a lower threshold is never accepted merely to fill the 90% sidebar. Produce MRL, xi and modified-scale beta(u)-xi*u stability, exceedance counts, QQ/PP, and risk sensitivity. Select/record the policy on development data; later fits use past data only at their origin. Raw beta need not be constant. Do not claim an uncalibrated fitted-GPD AD p-value.

For n observations and N_u strict exceedances, define a=(n/N_u)*(1-c):
VaR_c = u + beta/xi * (a**(-xi)-1).
For xi near0 use the stable exponential limit VaR_c=u-beta*log(a).
ES_c = (VaR_c+beta-xi*u)/(1-xi), only for xi<1.

For pure POT extrapolation require a<1, sufficient exceedances, positive beta and valid support. If xi>=1, ES is not finite; diagnose it. Never extrapolate GPD down into the body.

90/95/99 SUPPORT: implement an explicitly labelled empirical-body/GPD-tail distribution. Match tail mass p_u=N_u/n to the empirical body at u. Above u use the fitted GPD survival; below u use the empirical body and a stated quantile/boundary convention. At confidence below the tail mass boundary, VaR is a BODY estimate; ES must integrate the body segment plus the fitted upper tail, not blindly apply the pure-tail ES formula. This permits 90% with a defensible high threshold without pretending its VaR is pure EVT extrapolation. Record body/tail contribution and threshold in diagnostics/UI. Compare two justified threshold policies as sensitivity where feasible. Genuine bad fits, insufficient data or infinite ES remain explicit failures, not fabricated numbers.

A 500-day window has only about 25 exceedances at a 95% threshold, incompatible with minimum50. Use sufficient samples and actual counts in both recent and long-history views. Keep same-window comparisons; do not silently enlarge only EVT's sample. The recent window lacks GFC/COVID but includes 2022. The selected primary-model backtest may use its own registered 500-day rolling window; rolling EVT needs its own justified feasible window and is optional until implemented/tested.

Provide modest configurable block-bootstrap uncertainty for dependent financial losses, with reproducible seed, explicit block-length assumption and failed-fit counts. Cache it; report it as conditional on the threshold choice. A cheap iid parametric interval may additionally illustrate fit-only uncertainty if labelled; it does not account for volatility clustering or threshold-selection uncertainty. Never run bootstrap during every app rerun.

Tests: xi near0, xi>=1ES unavailable, bounded negative-xi support, too few exceedances, requested quantile below threshold, deterministic known-parameter quantiles, and seeded GPD parameter recovery with sensible statistical tolerance. Small isolated formula checks are more useful than a huge fragile simulation suite.

For the body/tail model also test normalisation, monotonic CDF/quantiles, threshold-boundary behaviour, agreement with pure POT in its valid domain and ES against numerical quantile integration. A body-region request is not a valid pure-tail request; tests must distinguish the two constructions rather than remove the safeguard.
